## Limitations
- Framing is based on existing OSM footprint, approximate source building height, and camera direction. It cannot guarantee photographing a precise architectural entrance; foliage may obscure views. The user has not approved photo-real building fidelity.
- No authentic iPhone Safari test, no precise field-survey heights, and no measured elevation terrain; mobile runs were Chromium/Edge touch simulation.
- Further photo-grounded building integrations should use only source-committed assets and avoid uncommitted north/south specialist worktrees.
